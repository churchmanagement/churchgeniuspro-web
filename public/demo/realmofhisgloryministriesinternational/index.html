Who we are

A church where you are already family

We are not a crowd that happens to meet on Sundays. We are a family — sons and daughters of God from dozens of cultures and backgrounds, learning together what it means to live under an open heaven.

Our name is our prayer. We long to see the realm of His glory made visible: in restored marriages, in healed bodies, in freed minds, in generous hands, and in a city that knows the goodness of Jesus because His people showed up.

  • Christ-centred worship that welcomes the presence of the Holy Spirit
  • Faithful, practical Bible teaching you can live out on Monday morning
  • Small groups and mentoring so no one walks their journey alone

What happens here

Ministries for every season of life

From your first visit to your first step of leadership, there is a place for you to belong, to grow and to serve.

Worship & Praise

A worship culture that makes room for the Holy Spirit — live choir, band and psalmists leading us into His presence every gathering.

The Word & Discipleship

Verse-by-verse teaching, mid-week Bible study and discipleship tracks that take you from first steps to firm foundations.

Prayer & Intercession

Corporate prayer, night watches and a standing intercession team that carries your requests before the Lord all week long.

Glory Kids

A bright, safe and secure environment where children ages 3–11 learn the Bible through story, song and creative play.

Youth & Young Adults

Teens and young adults finding real friendship, mentorship and a faith that holds up at school, at work and online.

Missions & Outreach

Feeding programmes, community partnerships and mission support that carry the love of Christ far beyond our walls.

New here?

Your first Sunday, made simple

No dress code. No pressure. Just a warm welcome and a seat saved for you.

01

Arrive a few minutes early

Our welcome team will meet you at the door, help you find parking and walk you to a seat — and to the Glory Kids check-in if you are bringing children.

02

Worship and the Word

Expect around 90 minutes together: live worship, a clear message from the Bible, and time to pray. Come as you are — jeans are as welcome as your Sunday best.

03

Stay for coffee

Meet a pastor at the Welcome Desk, ask anything, and let us pray with you. There is never an expectation to give as a guest.

Generosity

Every gift becomes someone’s good news
