Services & Ministries

Three gatherings a week, ministries for every age, and a family that is glad you came.

Weekly gatherings

When we meet

Every service is open to everyone. Bring a friend, bring a question, bring your family.

Sunday

Glory Service

Sundays · 10:00 AM

Our main weekly gathering — live worship, a clear message from the Bible, prayer ministry and communion on the first Sunday of each month.

  • Live worship team and choir
  • Expository teaching for everyday life
  • Prayer ministry available after every service
  • Glory Kids running alongside for ages 3–11

Wednesday

Word & Bible Study

Wednesdays · 7:00 PM

A mid-week deep dive. We work through a book of the Bible together, ask hard questions out loud, and leave with something to practise.

  • Verse-by-verse study, book by book
  • Round-table discussion and Q&A
  • Streamed live for those who cannot travel
  • Youth track meets at the same time

Friday

Night of Prayer

Fridays · 7:00 PM

Corporate intercession for our families, our city and the nations. Come with a burden, leave with a lighter one.

  • Guided corporate prayer
  • Personal prayer with an intercessor
  • Monthly extended night watch
  • Submit a request any time — we pray over every one

Planning your first visit

What to expect

The honest answers to the questions most guests are too polite to ask.

How long is a service?

About 90 minutes on Sunday. Doors open at 9:30 AM and coffee is served in the foyer before and after.

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see suits and you will see sneakers — both are welcome here.

What about my kids?

Glory Kids runs during the Sunday service for ages 3–11, with a secure check-in and background-checked volunteers. Infants are welcome in the service or the family room.

Where do I park?

Free on-site parking with marked guest and accessible bays near the main entrance. Our team will wave you in.

Find your place

Ministries and teams

You were not saved to sit. Whatever your gift, there is a team here that needs it.

Worship & Creative Arts

Choir, band, psalmists, media and production. Auditions and training run twice a year.

Glory Kids

Ages 3–11. Bible stories, worship, crafts and small groups in a safe, secure and joyful space.

Youth & Young Adults

Ages 12–25. Weekly hangs, mentoring, retreats and honest conversations about faith and real life.

Care & Counselling

Pastoral care, hospital and home visits, marriage and pre-marital support, and bereavement care.

Missions & Outreach

Food pantry, community care days, prison and hospital ministry, and overseas mission partnerships.

Intercession Team

A standing team praying through the week for every request submitted by our church family.

Glory Kids

Church your children look forward to

While you worship, your children are learning the same truths in a way they can hold on to — through story, song, games and small-group conversation.

  • Secure check-in and matching-tag pick-up at every service
