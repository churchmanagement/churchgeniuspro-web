Why we give

Worship you can measure

We do not give because the church needs money. We give because God has been extravagantly generous to us, and generosity is how that gets passed on.

Your giving keeps the lights on and the doors open. It funds the children’s ministry your kids love, the counselling a family is quietly receiving, the pantry shelves that get emptied every Saturday morning, and the missionaries we stand behind on three continents.

We take that trust seriously. Every gift is counted with dual oversight, recorded against your record, receipted annually, and reported to the church family once a year.

  • Secure, encrypted online giving — one-time or recurring
  • Annual contribution statements for every giver
  • Designated funds so you can give toward what moves you
  • Published annual financial report to the church family

Four ways to give

Give the way that works for you — every method reaches the same place.

1

Online

Good to know

Giving questions, answered

If your question is not here, the church office is glad to help.

Ask the church office

Call (281) 555-0142
Email hello@realmofhisglory.org

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Realm of His Glory Ministries International is a registered non-profit religious organisation. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and every giver receives an annual contribution statement.

Can I set up a recurring gift?

Yes. Choose weekly, monthly or one-time when you give online. Recurring gifts can be paused, changed or cancelled by you at any time.

Is online giving secure?

Giving is processed through an encrypted, PCI-compliant payment provider. The church never sees or stores your card details.

How is the money accounted for?

Our finance team follows a documented counting and reconciliation process with dual oversight, and the church family receives an annual financial report.

I am visiting — should I give?

Please do not feel any obligation. Giving is for those who call this church home. As our guest, the only thing we want from you is your company.

Can I give toward a specific need?

Yes. Select a fund when you give, or contact the church office and we will make sure your gift reaches exactly where you intend.
