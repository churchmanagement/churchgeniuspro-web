Our story

About Our Church

A house built on worship, the Word and the welcome of Jesus — serving our city and the nations since 2008.

How it began

From a living room to a family of nations

Realm of His Glory Ministries International began the way many good things do — with a handful of people, a few folding chairs, and a stubborn conviction that God still visits His people.

What started as a weekly prayer meeting in a small rented room grew into a congregation drawn from more than two dozen nations. Along the way the furniture changed, the address changed, and the sound system finally stopped cutting out. The heartbeat never changed: worship Him honestly, teach the Bible faithfully, and love people the way Jesus does.

Today we gather three times a week, run ministries for every age, and partner with organisations across our city and overseas. We are still a family that believes the next person through the door might be the reason God is doing all of this.

2008

A prayer meeting begins

Twelve believers commit to meeting weekly to pray for their families, their workplaces and their city.

2011

First Sunday service

The growing group holds its first public Sunday gathering and adopts the name Realm of His Glory Ministries International.

2015

Ministries take root

Glory Kids, the worship school and the mid-week Bible study launch, along with the first community food drive.

2019

A home of our own

The church moves into its current campus, with room for children’s ministry, counselling and a community pantry.

Today

Reaching further

Services stream worldwide, mission partnerships span three continents, and the doors are open every week.

Our Mission

To lead people into a living encounter with Jesus Christ, to establish them in the Word of God, and to release them to serve their generation with the gifts and grace God has given them.

Our Vision

A church without walls — where the glory of God rests on ordinary people, families are healed, communities are served, and disciples are sent from our city into the nations.

What we value

Six convictions that shape everything

These are not slogans on a wall. They decide how we plan a service, spend a dollar and treat a stranger.

Presence First

We would rather have His presence than a perfect programme. Everything we build makes room for the Holy Spirit to move.

Rooted in Scripture

We teach the whole counsel of God plainly and practically, so that faith becomes something you can stand on.

Family, Not Audience

Nobody sits anonymously here. We pursue real relationship, honest accountability and doors that stay open.

Compassion in Action

Love that never leaves the building is not love yet. We serve our neighbours before we are asked.

Every Nation Welcome

Our congregation reflects the nations. Different languages, one Lord, one table, one family.

Faithful Stewardship

We handle people, time and money with integrity, transparency and accountability — because it all belongs to God.

One thing I ask of the LORD — that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life.
Psalm 27:4

What we believe

Our statement of faith

We hold to the historic Christian faith as revealed in Scripture. These are the truths we build our lives on and teach without apology.

Have a question about what we believe?

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible and authoritative Word of God — our final rule for faith, doctrine and daily conduct.

One God, Three Persons

We believe in one eternal God who exists in three persons: God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Spirit, equal in power and glory.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, ascension and personal return in glory.

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is the gift of God received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — never earned, never deserved, freely given.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day ministry of the Holy Spirit, whose indwelling empowers the believer to live a godly life and to serve with spiritual gifts.

The Church & the Great Commission

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship, to make disciples of all nations, and to demonstrate the love of God in practical ways.
